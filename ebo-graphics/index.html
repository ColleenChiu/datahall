How it works

  1. You send what the job has. The point list or equipment list, your template or an existing screen to match, your naming rules, and the EBO version.
  2. You get a fixed quote within two working days. What you receive, in which format, by which date.
  3. One screen first. You approve the look on one screen before the rest of the set is built.
  4. Built offline, imported by your engineer. Files are delivered for your team to import into the project. I don't work on live servers or on site.
  5. Two revision rounds are included within the agreed scope.
